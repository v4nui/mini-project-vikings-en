## ⚔️🔥 Vikings vs Saxons: Battle for Glory! 🔥⚔️
### Welcome to Vikings vs Saxons, a battle simulator where you, the fearless commander, lead an army of Vikings or Saxons to claim ultimate victory on the battlefield!

### 🌍 The game brings you:

A sword-swinging good time with pixelated, battle-ready warriors.
Battle sequences where every swing, block, and clash could decide your fate.
A fun, strategic challenge where you manage your army and try to outlast the opponent.
Get ready to see your soldiers fight, fall, and rise again in this fun, casual adventure!

### 🎮 Features:
Viking vs. Saxon battles with unique combat styles.
Fun, lighthearted combat mechanics—no need to be a battle strategist, just enjoy the show!
Terminal-style battle updates so you can enjoy the ongoing drama of your warriors.

### ⚡ How to Play:
Set the size of your army (between 1 and 50 soldiers).
Click the "FIGHT" button and watch your army clash with the enemy!
Keep an eye on the terminal for live updates of the battle's progress.
Ready to get in on the action? Lead your warriors to victory and may the best army win!
